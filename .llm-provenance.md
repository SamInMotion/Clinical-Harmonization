## Critical Constraints
- BC5CDR data: public, cite Li et al. 2016
- No restricted clinical data in public repo
- PyTorch for model engineering (not just API calls)
- OMOP CDM v5.4 compliance for output
- Terminology versioning required (SNOMED-CT, ICD-10-CM, RxNorm, LOINC releases)
- The active EXP-010 vocabulary is deliberately incomplete and must not be described as comprehensive terminology coverage.
- Observation-domain concepts are not routed to OMOP destination tables.
- Unmapped mentions must be preserved rather than silently discarded or force-mapped.
- Current results do not establish OMOP CDM validity, FHIR conformance, clinical correctness, or deployment readiness.
- Historical invalid results must be retained and labeled, not deleted.
